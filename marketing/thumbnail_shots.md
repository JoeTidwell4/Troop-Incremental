# Thumbnail + icon shot list

Why: your ad got ~1% clicks. The thumbnail and icon are most of that.
Where to change: Creator Dashboard > your game > **Places > Thumbnails** (images 1920x1080) and **Icon** (512x512).

## Thumbnails (take 3, put the best first)
1. **Boss fight**: a big glowing boss (aura on) in the middle, your army in front of it, mid-attack (sparks, arrows). Best: the Hollow King or the Magma Colossus.
2. **Huge army**: 300+ of a few troop types (gold trim, capes, glowing blades), camera low so the army fills the frame.
3. **Story moment**: a cutscene shot with the letterbox bars + a dramatic line (the Hollow King appearing works).

Tips
- Hide the HUD for clean shots: while playing in Studio, open the Explorer > Players > (your name) > PlayerGui and untick **Enabled** on the ScreenGuis you don't want (HUD, shop). Tick them back after.
- Add 2-4 words of big bold text on top in any image editor (Canva is free): e.g. "BUILD YOUR ARMY", "DEFEAT THE HOLLOW KING".
- Bright and readable at tiny size. No small text, no busy UI.

## Icon (512x512)
- One clear thing: a Knight's helmet with the glowing visor, OR the Hollow Crown, on a strong color background.
- Same color as your thumbnails so they feel like one game.
- Check it at phone size before uploading.

## Ad creative
Use your best thumbnail as the ad image too. Run the ad again once these are in, and compare the click rate (was ~1%).
