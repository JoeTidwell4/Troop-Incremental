# TikTok clips (5 ideas)

How to record on Windows: play in Studio, **Win + Alt + R** starts/stops (clips go to Videos > Captures).
On Mac: **Cmd + Shift + 5** > Record Selected Portion.
Make the window tall and narrow if you can (TikTok is vertical). Keep the TEST / ADMIN buttons out of the shot.
Put the text hook on screen in the first second. 7-20 seconds each. Caption: game name + "link in bio".
Post 3-5 and see which one gets views before making more of that kind.

## 1. "Day 1 vs 300 Swordsmen" (~15 s)
- Hook text: **"My army on day 1 vs day 7"**
- Shots: 1 lonely Swordsman fighting -> quick cuts as the count goes 25 / 100 / 300 (armor changes each time) -> the gold-trim army flattening a boss.
- Setup: TEST > gold buttons, buy Swordsmen in steps.

## 2. "This boss took me 6 tries" (~20 s)
- Hook: **"This boss took me 6 tries"**
- Shots: losing (army falls), the "WEAKENED -30%" tag, hitting the glowing weak points, the boss's death + loot burst.
- Setup: TEST > Stage (pick a stage just before a boss, e.g. 49), TEST > Fail boss a few times.

## 3. "POV: a golden enemy spawns" (~8 s)
- Hook: **"POV: you find a golden enemy"**
- Shots: the gold glow, the kill, "GOLDEN! +gold", "+1 free gear pack".
- Setup: TEST > Golden enemy.

## 4. "I pulled a MYTHIC" (~10 s)
- Hook: **"1% chance... and I got it"** (only if you actually got it on camera)
- Shots: the recruit reveal: chest shaking, rays, the card popping in.
- Setup: TEST > +20 Recruit Scrolls, record a few x10 pulls, keep the best.

## 5. "The final boss" (~20 s)
- Hook: **"The final boss of my game"**
- Shots: the Hollow King rising in the intro, a taunt line, the fight, the crown shattering in the ending.
- Setup: TEST > Story > Skip to the final battle.

Every clip: end on the army or the boss mid-hit (loops better than a menu).
