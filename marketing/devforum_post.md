**What it is**
- Your troops fight automatically. You click to help, use abilities, and spend gold on more troops and better gear.
- 6 troop types (Swordsmen to Siege engines). Each one gets new armor as you hire more of them.
- Every 10th stage is a boss with real mechanics: shields, summoned minions, rage mode, weak points to hit.
- A full story mode, *The Hollow King*: 10 chapters with cutscenes, ending in a final battle.
- Your camp is the menu. You click the tents, carts and boards around the battlefield instead of a wall of buttons.
- Lots to dig into later: Generals to recruit, relics, a skill tree, an endless tower, challenge runs, expeditions, clans with raids and a weekly clan war, and two prestige layers.
