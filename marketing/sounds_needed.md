# Sounds: the 12 that matter most

The game has ~35 sound slots, but these 12 make most of the difference. Send me the IDs
in chat like `Hit = 123456789` and I'll paste them in.

| Slot | When it plays | What to search for |
|---|---|---|
| Hit | every click on an enemy (plays A LOT: pick something short and soft) | "sword hit", "punch light" |
| Kill | an enemy dies | "pop", "coin", "enemy death" |
| UIClick | every button | "ui click", "button" |
| Buy | you train troops | "coin purchase", "cash register" |
| Upgrade | gear level up | "level up", "upgrade" |
| Reward | claiming rewards | "reward", "success chime" |
| BossStart | a boss fight begins | "war horn", "boss start" |
| BossWin | you beat a boss | "victory", "fanfare" |
| BossFail | you lose a boss | "fail", "defeat" |
| BossRoar | a boss arrives / enrages | "monster roar" |
| PackRare | a Rare+ pack / chest reveal | "magic sparkle", "legendary" |
| Boss (music) | boss fight music | "epic battle music", "boss music" |

## How to get free sounds (Studio)
1. In Studio: **View > Toolbox**.
2. In the Toolbox, switch the category to **Audio**.
3. Search the words from the table. Click the play button to listen.
4. Prefer sounds made by **Roblox** (they're free to use in any game). Other people's audio
   may be private and won't play in your game.
5. To get the ID: right-click the sound > **Copy Asset ID**. Paste it in chat with its slot name.

Tip: short and not too loud for Hit, Kill and UIClick. Those play hundreds of times.
